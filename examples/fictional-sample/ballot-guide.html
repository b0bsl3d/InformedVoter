Every contest on this ballot, in exact ballot order. Contest numbers match the ballot cheat sheet. Contested races get a full box; uncontested races are one line in their ballot position. This guide is nonpartisan and recommends no candidate or vote.

First time voting? What to know in Example State
  • Photo ID required: driver's license, state ID, passport or military ID. Without one you can cast a provisional ballot and show ID within 6 days. source
  • Early voting runs Oct. 19–30 at any county vote center. Election Day hours are 7 a.m.–7 p.m.; if you're in line at 7 p.m., you can still vote. source
  • There is no straight-party option. Mark each contest separately; skipping a contest doesn't affect the rest of your ballot. source
  • You may bring printed notes. Phones may not be used at the voting station. source
